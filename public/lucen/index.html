The detector

Sleep paralysis has an unusual signature: the body is locked still while the heart and breathing race. A nightmare brings twitching and turning over is a short burst of movement. The model separates the three.

Watch sensorsHeart rate, breathing estimate and motion, once a second
Rolling windowLast 10 to 20 s against the person's own baseline
Seven featuresHeart lift, slope, breathing lift, irregularity, movement, stillness
Onset scorerLogistic model, a score from 0 to 1 each second
Persistence ruleAbove threshold for several seconds, then a 2 minute lockout
InterventionThree haptic stages, lights and partner alert by plan

This v0 scorer stands in for the production attention model. It is small enough to run on the watch, which keeps raw biometrics off our servers.

What the model learned

Read it like this: a climbing heart rate, irregular breathing and a still body score high. Any burst of movement pulls the score down hard, which is how it ignores you rolling over.

The weak spot is the nightmare. Heart rate and breathing spike just like an episode and only the twitching tells them apart. Roughly 1 in 20 simulated nightmares still trigger a buzz. Real labelled nights and sleep-stage context are the fix.

What this demo is

  • A working end-to-end MVP: signal stream, onset detection, staged intervention, plan gating and clinician export.
  • Real haptics on an Android phone, and a sound cue on a laptop.
  • Every business figure here is computed live from the same model as the Assignment IV workbook.

What it is not yet

  • Not validated on real sleepers. Every signal is simulated.
  • Not a medical device. It is positioned as wellness until a clinical study says otherwise.
  • The wake-up delay is an assumption the pilot will measure.

Module 2 · Revenue

Plans and pricing

A free tier earns trust with tracking. The first paid tier is the core promise: real-time detection and wake-up. Pro adds what a partner or a clinician cares about. Pick a plan and the rest of this prototype changes with it.

What each plan unlocks

Try it. Pick Free, open Tonight and run an episode. Lucen logs it but cannot intervene. Switch to Core and the same night gets haptics. Switch to Pro and the room lights come up too. That gap is the upgrade path.
